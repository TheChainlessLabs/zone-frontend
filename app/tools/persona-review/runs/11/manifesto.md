Trust is a workflow, not an aesthetic. A fund operator opens this app on a shared screen at a Tuesday risk meeting; they have to be able to walk a colleague through how it matched, how it sealed, how it settled, and how to retrieve the receipt three quarters from now. /batches is the institutional muscle of this build — it speaks the right vocabulary. Everything else is consumer-shaped: a wallet pill that doesn't disclose connector or session age, an /account page with no audit log, deposit/withdrawal modals that hide the chain confirmation, an `0x...` settlement hash that's a span instead of a link. Quiet down the celebration colours, surface the receipt, and write copy a compliance reviewer can quote.
