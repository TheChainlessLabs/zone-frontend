# Amira Haddad — inspirations

External references consulted during this review. Grouped by what they teach an institutional execution surface.

## Institutional digital-finance — the venue register

- [Goldman Sachs Marquee — platform overview](https://marquee.gs.com/welcome/our-platform/overview) — what we take: the contract-header order ticket. The trade restates itself as static text above the action; the action is neutral.
- [Goldman Sachs Marquee — Marquee Trader desktop](https://marquee.gs.com/welcome/products/access-points/desktop) — what we take: the ticket-blotter-audit composition. Every visible thing is a row in a record, not a card with chrome.
- [J.P. Morgan Markets — institutional platform](https://www.jpmorgan.com/markets) — what we take: the five-pillar shape (research, data, portfolio, pricing, post-trade) and the recent overhaul that prioritises trade contract over decoration.
- [J.P. Morgan Markets next-generation platform (FinTech Magazine)](https://fintechmagazine.com/news/jpmorgan-markets-launches-institutional-platform-overhaul) — what we take: the design brief — reduce navigation time, surface the contract, prioritise the institutional reader.
- [UBS Family Office and UHNW solutions](https://www.ubs.com/global/en/wealthmanagement/family-office-uhnw/family-office-solutions.html) — what we take: how a wealth surface opens with a date stamp and an allocation list, not a marketing line. The audit anchor is the subhead.
- [UBS digital experience](https://www.ubs.com/us/en/wealth-management/our-approach/digital-experience.html) — what we take: the reference for "private wealth credibility" — restraint, monochrome wallet/account marks, full notional rendered as primary text.

## Typographic and brand register — the seriousness signal

- [Goldman Sachs Design System — Typography](https://design.gs.com/foundation/typography) — what we take: the GS Sans / GS Serif / Sabon pairing. Wide-sans for tabular numerics, serif used sparingly for headlines, never as a section subhead. The Source Serif 4 italic accent in Omega should follow this discipline — spent once, never decorative.
- [Goldman Sachs visual identity update — restoring classic typography (1000logos)](https://1000logos.net/news/goldman-sachs-updates-its-visual-identity-restoring-classic-typography/) — what we take: the precedent that institutional rebrands move *toward* serif gravity, not away from it. Validates Omega's serif accent at the architectural level.
- [The Curious Case of Goldman Sans (PRINT Magazine)](https://www.printmag.com/branding-identity-design/the-curious-case-of-goldman-sans/) — what we take: the design intent — readability of large quantities of small numbers, optical balance of digits, alphabetic-numeric harmony. The discipline a darkpool ticket needs.

## Quiet luxury — restraint as posture

- [Hermès quiet luxury (Kantar)](https://www.kantar.com/inspiration/agile-market-research/why-hermes-quiet-luxury-speaks-loudest) — what we take: pricing and material as confidence signals; the marketing philosophy of "saying less accomplishes more". The institutional finance translation — let the proof claim and the audit row speak; don't tint the action button.
- [Aesop typography and brand voice (Fonts In Use)](https://fontsinuse.com/uses/20234/aesop-logo-website-and-packaging) — what we take: utilitarian labels, calm typographic register, instructions over slogans. The closest commercial-product analogue for what a settlement-instruction modal (deposit, withdraw, sign) should read like.
- [Quiet design — luxury brands redefining power through restraint (ICON Magazine)](https://www.iconeye.com/sponsored-content/quiet-design-how-luxury-brands-are-redefining-power-through-restraint) — what we take: the broader cultural argument that restraint is the premium signal, not chrome. Used to defend the kill list against decorative-donut and tinted-CTA defenders.

## Apple — high-trust product clarity

- [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/) — what we take: clarity, deference, hierarchy. Sufficient text contrast (4.5:1 baseline). Adornments kept to a minimum. Neutral primary actions. The reference for the redesigned CTA register.
- [Apple sufficient contrast evaluation criteria (Developer)](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-accessibility/sufficient-contrast-evaluation-criteria/) — what we take: the institutional baseline that available balance, fee, and price must all meet at full weight, not at `text-[10px]`.

## Audit-trail discipline — what an institutional reviewer expects to read

- [FINRA Consolidated Audit Trail (CAT)](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/guidance/reports/2023-finras-examination-and-risk-monitoring-program/cat) — what we take: the regulatory frame — every event UTC, millisecond precision, NIST-synchronised. The audit row Omega's ticket is currently missing.
- [Trading Technologies — Audit Trail reference](https://library.tradingtechnologies.com/trade/at-reference.html) — what we take: the visible format — ISO-8601 UTC, sequencer identifier per row, full transaction lineage. The format I want printed inline on /batches detail and on /portfolio fills.
- [SIFMA Firm's Guide to the Consolidated Audit Trail (PDF)](https://www.sifma.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/SIFMA-Firms-CAT-Guide.pdf) — what we take: the compliance officer's checklist. Validates the "treasury reviewer brings CAT expectations to any execution surface" framing in the critique.

## Anti-references — the surfaces a treasury committee will not approve

- [Pump.fun economic and legal analysis (Storm Partners)](https://storm.partners/blog-post/meme-coin-mania-on-pump-fun-an-economic-and-legal-analysis) — what we take *as warning*: gamified celebration UX (tinted CTAs, casino register, instant launch) is now the explicit precedent regulators (MiCAR, U.S. AML/gambling) cite. A green-tinted Buy button is regulator-adjacent. A compliance officer will not approve a venue whose action surface visually rhymes with this platform.
- [Pump.fun — Wikipedia overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pump.fun) — what we take: the historical record. Anonymous developer, "most fun place on the internet", $1B revenue days, casino-aesthetic branding. The surface a private-capital allocator must be visibly *not*.
- [Binance design language summary (getdesign.md)](https://getdesign.md/binance/design-md) — what we take *as warning*: bold yellow accents on monochrome surfaces, trading-floor urgency. Powerful but loud. The retail-crypto register Omega has to walk away from to be approved for treasury allocation.

## Notes on sourcing

Two of the persona's reference sites — `ubs.com`, `apple.com` — return generic search abstracts, not deep design specs. That is expected: institutional and luxury vendor sites do not publish their design systems. The Goldman Design System site (`design.gs.com`) is the exception and is cited where it matters. The remaining references extend the persona's floor with sibling institutional, audit-trail, and quiet-luxury sources.
