Motion should stay below the user's pulse. Omega is strongest when the trade form holds still, the lifecycle advances in one inevitable line, and proof appears as evidence rather than applause. I want fewer duplicated signals, one shared rhythm across desktop and mobile, and no transition that asks for attention unless it improves comprehension. The standard is simple: if movement helps a trader understand where an order, batch, or proof is in its path, keep it; if it tries to create urgency, delight, or reward, remove it.
