Omega does not need a louder room. It needs a quieter one. Right now the page is two competing surfaces — a SaaS dot-grid behind, a stack of soft-shadowed cards in front — and the actual product is the third loudest thing on screen. A dark-pool desk should read like a Norm Architects entryway: hairline, recess, restraint, and one piece of editorial italic positioned where the eye must rest. Drop the dot-grid to a whisper, retire one of every two card frames, demote the success-emerald from accent to atmosphere, and let Source Serif carry the lede only where a magazine standfirst would carry it. Silence is the feature institutional buyers are paying for. Confidence is what's left when you stop announcing it.
