# African payment utility

- https://www.safaricom.co.ke/personal/m-pesa/lipa-na-m-pesa/m-pesa-business-till
  What we take: the rail explains who can pay, where funds can go next, and how to operate it from app or USSD without assuming premium hardware.
- https://www.safaricom.co.ke/main-mpesa/for-your-business/business-paybill
  What we take: payment collection is framed as secure, timely, low-cost business utility with real-time reports, not fintech theatre.
- https://flutterwave.com/us/send-money
  What we take: payouts are described in business terms first: vendors, payroll, countries, verification, bulk transfer.
- https://paystack.com/docs/transfers/managing-transfers/
  What we take: show balance checks, approval gates, and queued states before pretending money movement is simple.
- https://paystack.com/docs/transfers/how-transfers-work/
  What we take: `pending`, `success`, and `reversed` are operator language; Omega should borrow that discipline.
- https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2130114
  What we take: balances and top-ups deserve their own explicit surface because available money is the first operational question.
- https://support.paystack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009881720-How-do-I-make-bulk-transfers-from-my-Paystack-Dashboard
  What we take: the dashboard shows balance, status, recipients, filters, and export because businesses need a control panel, not just a CTA.
- https://www.chippercash.com/chipper-checkout
  What we take: cross-border payment UX earns trust by collapsing collection and corridor context into a few direct actions.
- https://www.chippercash.com/send-and-receive
  What we take: if movement is easy, say it plainly and keep the send path short.
- https://www.opayweb.com/
  What we take: control of funds, transfers, and bill-pay utility should be legible in the first screenful.

# Stablecoin and treasury rails

- https://yellowcard.io/api-suite
  What we take: stablecoin infrastructure has to explain local currency coverage, payout rails, wallet options, and transparent fees in one place.
- https://help.yellowcard.io/article/1016-api-documentation
  What we take: transaction status, history, corridor support, and settlement in stablecoins are product features, not back-office footnotes.
- https://yellowcard.io/
  What we take: serious emerging-market finance speaks in currencies, countries, compliance, and treasury operations.

# Anti-references

- https://www.lseg.com/en/data-analytics/products/workspace
  What we take: this is what happens when a product assumes deep desks, large screens, installation guides, and layered workflows; Omega should not import that weight into mobile.
- https://www.bloomberg.com/professional/solution/bloomberg-terminal/
  What we take: powerful, yes, but built for always-on professional environments with monitors, alerts, and research layers; wrong default for a phone-first settlement rail.
