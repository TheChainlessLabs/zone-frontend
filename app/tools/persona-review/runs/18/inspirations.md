## Finance operations references

- https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28767842120852-Understanding-your-Mercury-balances — Break the top line into usable balance states or the total is operationally meaningless.
- https://mercury.com/blog/updated-transactions-page — The transactions table is the product, not a support act under a hero chart.
- https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/38790547830036-Viewing-cashflow-and-transactions-data-on-your-Transactions-page — Filters, grouping, and saved views should help me close books faster, not browse prettier.
- https://www.brex.com/product/spend-management/ — A finance dashboard earns trust when it behaves like one source of truth for controls and review.
- https://support.ramp.com/hc/en-us/articles/4417421399699-Reviewing-transactions-from-Ramp-cards — Reviewable items must be separated from merely pending ones.
- https://support.ramp.com/hc/en-us/articles/44976060889619-Expenses-transactions-and-reimbursements-filters — Operational state needs explicit tabs and filters, not buried labels.
- https://www.moderntreasury.com/products/ledgers — Every money movement should read like an immutable entry, not an aesthetic artifact.
- https://docs.moderntreasury.com/reconciliation/docs/account-reconciliation-overview — Show variance and resolution paths directly where money state is reviewed.
- https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/bank-transactions/account-registers-quickbooks-online/L8V2Hal1f_US_en_US — The register is the accountability surface.
- https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/statement-reconciliation/reconcile-account-quickbooks-online/L3XzsllsK_US_en_US — Reconciliation is only finished when the difference goes to zero.
- https://docs.treasuryprime.com/reference/account — Accounts should expose balances and transactions plainly enough that nobody has to guess the model.

## Market infrastructure references

- https://docs.polymarket.com/market-makers/trading — Useful reminder that order mechanics alone are not enough for a treasury-facing portfolio surface.

## Anti-references

- https://rainbow.me/en — Consumer-wallet delight and colorfulness are the wrong instincts for balance accountability.
- https://support.zora.co/en/articles/6316801 — Turning identity into the tradable object pushes the product toward social speculation, not treasury clarity.
- https://app.pump.fun/ — Anything that smells like trend-chasing breaks the seriousness required for six-figure settlement workflows.

## Why these matter for Omega

- Omega can keep the dark infrastructure tone, but the portfolio page has to graduate from "what do I hold?" to "what cleared, what is locked, what is provable, and what can leave right now?"
