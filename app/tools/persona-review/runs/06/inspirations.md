## Motion hierarchy

- https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion
  What we take from it: motion is only useful when it explains continuity, hierarchy, or outcome.
- https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets
  What we take from it: modal surfaces should preserve the user's sense of place instead of arriving like separate scenes.
- https://m3.material.io/styles/motion/overview
  What we take from it: timing needs a small, explicit ladder or teams start improvising.
- https://www.framer.com/help/articles/how-animations-and-effects-work-in-framer/
  What we take from it: layout change should read as one relationship updating, not several boxes entering independently.
- https://www.framer.com/help/articles/reduced-motion-settings/
  What we take from it: reduced motion is not an afterthought; it is part of the same design decision.

## Product restraint

- https://www.sony.com/en/SonyDesign/
  What we take from it: product presence comes from proportion and timing, not effects.
- https://www.sony.com/en/SonyInfo/design/stories/motionlogo/
  What we take from it: movement can settle into form quietly and still feel deliberate.
- https://www.muji.com/us/about-muji/
  What we take from it: utility speaks best when the interface removes explanation that the object can carry itself.
- https://www.playstation.com/en-us/ps5/features/
  What we take from it: layered context can feel cinematic without losing control when the primary task stays fixed.

## Anti-references

- https://www.binance.com/en
  What we take from it: too many simultaneous invitations make a trading surface feel promotional before it feels trustworthy.
- https://www.okx.com/
  What we take from it: animation without silence becomes another notification system.
- https://dexscreener.com/
  What we take from it: scan-heavy market-board energy is the wrong behavioural model for institutional settlement pages.

## Behaviour cost precedent

- https://www.cnbc.com/2020/12/17/robinhood-puts-away-digital-confetti-as-regulators-cite-gamification-concerns.html
  What we take from it: celebratory financial feedback can create the wrong regulatory and emotional reading.
- https://www.cnbc.com/2020/12/16/massachusetts-sec-o-commonwealth-galvin-says-robinhood-is-a-reckless-company-gamifying-investing.html
  What we take from it: gamification is not merely a taste problem; it changes how a market product is judged.
- https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/securities/robinhood/robinhood-index.htm
  What we take from it: there is a documented cost when interface mechanics are seen as steering behaviour rather than clarifying action.
