An institutional FX product is judged at its edges, not its happy path. A keyboard-only trader on a screen reader must connect, fund, place a market order, hear the outcome, and find that order again — without crypto fluency and without a guess. Today the OrderForm has no error summary, the proof-state pills carry no plain-language consequence, the portfolio chart has no non-visual equivalent, and the wrong-network banner sits above the navbar where the tab order buries it. Strip the 10px uppercase eyebrow type, replace label-stacks with `<dl>` semantics, attach `aria-describedby` to every input, announce state changes through `aria-live`, and write every error in two clauses: what happened, what to do next. If it excludes, it fails.
