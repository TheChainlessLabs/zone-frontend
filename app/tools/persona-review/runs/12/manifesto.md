Perp traders do not want a tasteful form floating in space. They want a desk: fixed zones, instant pair switching, a ticket that treats market and limit like different weapons, and a visible explanation of why private midpoint matching is better than a public book for this venue. Omega already understands privacy; it does not yet express trading muscle memory. Move the execution model up, move post-submit management next to the ticket, and stop borrowing dashboard or transfer-product spacing where speed should dominate.
