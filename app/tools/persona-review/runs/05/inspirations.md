# Inspirations — Persona 05, Luca Bianchi

What the eye took from each page, in this persona's voice.

## Editorial canon — financial papers of record

- **García Media — *Financial Times: a classic redesign for the digital age*** — https://garciamedia.com/blog/financial_times_a_classic_redesign_for_the_digital_age/
  Take: the move from 8-col to 6-col, the analysis-vs-news typographic split (centred + ranged-left as editorial register), and the bespoke Financier family doing the persuading. Apply to /batches: the page is analysis, not a feed.

- **Klim Type Foundry — *Financial Times in use*** — https://klim.co.nz/in-use/financial-times/
  Take: Financier Display + Financier Text + Metric is three faces with three jobs and no overlap. Our analogue — Geist Sans + Geist Mono + Source Serif 4 italic — only works if the italic is reserved for one role and held there.

- **Fountn — *The Economist visual style guide*** — https://fountn.design/resource/the-economist-visual-style-guide/
  Take: Milo Serif for body, Econ Sans for chrome, "no-nonsense" stroke uniformity in headers. The principle to steal: the standfirst is a sentence, not a stat band, and figures are inline citizens of the prose.

- **24 Ore System — *Il Sole 24 Ore digital platform*** — https://24oresystem.ilsole24ore.com/en/brands/il-sole-24-ore-newspaper/
  Take: a markets section ranked Top Newspaper for trustworthiness eight years running (Reuters Digital News Report). Trustworthiness is a layout output, not a copy claim. /batches inherits this responsibility.

- **TypeMates — *NZZ.ch relaunch*** — https://www.typemates.com/news/nzz-ch-relaunch
  Take: Pensum Pro for long-form, GT America for chrome — restraint as identity. The "austere visage" of NZZ is the model for an attestation log: nothing decorative, everything carrying weight.

- **Nieman Journalism Lab — *How NZZ cultivates paying audiences*** — https://www.niemanlab.org/2016/07/how-the-swiss-newspaper-nzz-is-building-products-to-try-and-cultivate-new-paying-audiences/
  Take: editorial product thinking — the page is a product whose job is to *earn* its reader, not to satisfy a database query.

- **magCulture — *Monocle, redesigned*** — https://magculture.com/blogs/journal/monocle-redesigned
  Take: looser grids, more white, fewer images, more words. /batches is a words-first surface; the rebuild leans on standfirst prose, not on stat tiles.

## Data-as-narrative — Bloomberg-style annotation

- **Anychart — *New examples of visual storytelling in action*** — https://www.anychart.com/blog/2026/01/23/visual-storytelling-examples/
  Take: Bloomberg "Big Take" scrollytelling — annotations build the network, not the chart's defaults. Translate: each /batches entry is a one-sentence annotation of one sealed proof, not a row of unlabelled markers.

- **Storytelling with Charts — *Context is key: annotations and labels effectively*** — https://www.storytellingwithcharts.com/blog/context-is-key-using-data-visualization-annotation-and-labels-effectively/
  Take: well-placed annotations beat unlabelled visuals on comprehension — a reader prefers a chart with words over a chart without. Apply: per-row standfirst sentence beats per-row chip rack.

- **GIJN — *From data to storytelling: tips from FT's John Burn-Murdoch*** — https://gijn.org/stories/data-visualization-storytelling-tips-john-burn-murdoch/
  Take: a chart's narrative title and explanatory text do most of the comprehension work. Translate: the eyebrow + italic dek on each editorial state earns its space because it does the explaining, not the visual.

## Swiss / structural lineage

- **Müller-Brockmann — *Grid Systems in Graphic Design* (PDF)** — https://monoskop.org/images/a/a4/Mueller-Brockmann_Josef_Grid_Systems_in_Graphic_Design_Raster_Systeme_fuer_die_Visuele_Gestaltung_English_German_no_OCR.pdf
  Take: white space is purposeful, not residual; the grid is a system of order that makes the message understood. The marginalia rail and the run-of-paper column come straight out of this.

- **Swiss Themes — *Swiss design principles every web designer should know*** — https://swissthemes.design/insights/swiss-design-for-web-designers
  Take: type as spatial material, not ornament; alignment to the grid is the carrier of clarity. Apply to ordinal numbering and left-margin status ticks.

## Anti-references (cost-framed)

- **Rondesignlab — *TradingView platform UX case study*** — https://rondesignlab.com/cases/tradingview-platform-for-traders
  Cost: cluttered, overloaded dashboards "slow down analysis and decision-making"; fix was "simplifying chart control." For a public attestation log, the cost is worse — clutter makes the page feel like a feed and not a record. Stay short of this.

- **CoinMarketCap — homepage** — https://coinmarketcap.com
  Cost: ranking-page energy. Every cell competes with every other cell. Not the energy a sealed-proof ledger should carry.

- **TradingView Indicators marketplace** — https://www.tradingview.com/scripts/
  Cost: density without hierarchy. /batches must not become a script-marketplace shelf.
