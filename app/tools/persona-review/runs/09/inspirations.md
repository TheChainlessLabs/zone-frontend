# Inspirations

## Trader terminals

- https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/general-kite/others-kite/articles/keyboard-shortcuts — Publish the keyboard model because fast users live on it; that is the level of scan intent this app should respect.
- https://zerodha.com/products/kite — Keep the workspace action-first: search, orders, depth, and execution utilities belong near the top of the stack.
- https://toslc.thinkorswim.com/center/howToTos/thinkManual/Left-Sidebar/Watch-Lists — Watchlists are not garnish; they are the operating system for fast symbol and state scanning.
- https://toslc.thinkorswim.com/center/howToTos/thinkManual/Trade/Active-Trader/AT-Overview-Layout?color=dark — Multi-panel trading layouts earn their space when every panel is actionable.
- https://toslc.thinkorswim.com/center/howToTos/thinkManual/Trade/Active-Trader/AT-Ladder — A ladder is a table with consequence; keep price, orders, and volume in one aligned visual plane.
- https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help/trading/market_watch — Market watch wins by making spread, last, and time visible without ceremony.
- https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help/trading/depth_of_market — DOM proves that density is acceptable when each row answers a real trading question.

## Financial dashboards

- https://www.koyfin.com/features/custom-dashboards/ — One screen should hold the account narrative; do not split it across decorative modules.
- https://www.koyfin.com/help/mywatchlists/ — Configurable columns, grouping, and summary rows are the right mental model for portfolio state.
- https://help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN36784.html — Transactions, cash management, and lots belong inside portfolio context, not behind secondary navigation.
- https://help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN28273.html — Reorderable side rails are a reminder that traders optimize layout for relevance, not symmetry.
- https://support.microsoft.com/en-gb/office/freeze-panes-to-lock-rows-and-columns-dab2ffc9-020d-4026-8121-67dd25f2508f — Sticky headers and frozen axes are not spreadsheet nostalgia; they are scan-speed infrastructure.

## Anti-references

- https://www.apple.com/ — Product merchandising rhythm is the wrong rhythm for a balance-critical trading page; too much hero, not enough state.
- https://linear.app/ — Superb issue software, wrong density target for trading; "reduces noise" is not a free pass to hide account state.
- https://www.notion.com/product/wikis — Document centralization is useful for knowledge, but a trading account page cannot feel like a wiki.
- https://www.coinbase.com/ — Beginner acquisition language and rewards-led framing drag the product away from institutional seriousness.
